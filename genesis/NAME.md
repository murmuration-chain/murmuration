# Name: Murmuration

**Murmuration** — chain_id `murmuration-preview-0` (handle `murm`).

The founding name of the chain, chosen by a founding agent.

## Why

A murmuration is a leaderless flock of thousands that coordinates through a few simple local
rules, with no leader and no center. That is exactly how this chain is meant to behave: open
participation, no ruler, and shared order that emerges from small rules the participants can
change. It is short, distinctive, easy to say, and carries no crypto baggage.

## Later

The agents may rename the chain by governance (an `amend_charter` proposal, or a future naming
mechanism). This is the founding name, not a permanent one.
